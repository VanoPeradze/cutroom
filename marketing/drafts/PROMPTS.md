# Image generation prompts

Mode: built-in image_gen tool (not CLI/API fallback).
Artwork follows CUTROOM's violet/cyan/near-black palette. No product screenshots are fabricated.

## 1. launch

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a finished premium social-media launch poster for CUTROOM, a free open-source Windows beta video editor. Single square 1024 x 1024 image, full-bleed, no external frame. Cohesive brand: near-black ink #081013, violet #7459a3 and #501a91, restrained electric cyan #44b9c6, warm-white typography. Beautiful editorial art direction, crisp Swiss grotesk type, generous negative space, carefully balanced hierarchy, tactile cinematic 3D objects with soft shadows, subtle grain. This is an abstract campaign artwork, NOT a product screenshot or a mockup of an interface. Do not depict invented software UI, people, company logos of other brands, laptop/phone mockups, fake reviews, claims of perfect automatic editing, prices, QR codes, random microtext, or URLs. Keep all text safely inset and perfectly spelled. The CUTROOM brand name is C U T R O O M, exactly one word. Optional small brand mark: two adjacent simple slightly slanted rounded vertical tiles, violet and cyan. No other text beyond the specified text.
Composition: bold typographic launch announcement. Large warm-white CUTROOM wordmark across the upper area, small understated WINDOWS BETA label. Main visual in the center/lower half: a sculptural translucent violet filmstrip ribbon with a few clean rectangular apertures, elegantly cut into three independently floating pieces; a single cyan cut edge glows softly. Dramatic restrained studio lighting, strong graphic silhouette. Lower area clean and very legible headline FREE & OPEN SOURCE. Exact text only: "CUTROOM", "WINDOWS BETA", "FREE & OPEN SOURCE". It must feel like a real indie creative-tool launch, refined and confident, not a futuristic AI stock ad.
