# CUTROOM / The screening room

## Surface and direction

The public, bilingual CUTROOM marketing and download site uses **Persuade** mode. The visitor should understand the local editor, see genuine product evidence and choose the appropriate download without hunting for the action. This is a new visual world, not a cosmetic pass on the previous cool-white and mint composition.

The opening is a graphite screening room with copper light. Oversized, left-aligned editorial type sits beside an original perspective field of film frames. Warm paper sections provide a deliberate change of pace. The cyan/violet CUTROOM mark remains the product signature. No neon green, unrelated visual assets, fabricated testimonials, inflated performance claims or simulated product controls are used.

## Composition and interaction

- Opaque sticky navigation preserves the section anchors, language choice and download action.
- The opening pairs the product statement with the exact Windows and Mac package links. Beta status and the Mac source-launcher distinction are visible before download.
- The single authored motion moment is original canvas geometry behind the opening, not invented editor footage. A visible pause control is available immediately.
- An actual, unmodified editor screenshot provides product evidence. Its October capture date and archival status remain explicit. Enlargement opens its original resolution.
- Varied format studies introduce vertical, landscape and manual editing without pretending to be application controls.
- Media, Edit and Output steps lead to the matching working gallery tabs.
- The ten-workspace tour retains keyboard access, RTL behavior, original-image links and dialog focus return. Download invitations recur after the tour and at the end without blocking content.
- Optional AI choices, installation prerequisites, known limits, release evidence and privacy remain findable. This is not a claim that providers or local models are configured.

## Typography and palette

Geist and Noto Sans Hebrew are self-hosted under their shipped SIL Open Font Licenses. Display type is capped at 96px, tracking never tighter than -0.04em. Body copy and controls use proportional type, never monospace as decoration.

- Graphite: `#141619`; panel: `#202328`; dividing line: `#45474d`.
- Main text: `#f5f1e9`; secondary text: `#c9c7c3`; muted: `#adaeaf`.
- Copper: `#efb28d`; dark button text: `#201c19`; violet accent: `#b7a6de`.
- Warm paper: `#f0eee8`; paper text: `#28282d`; paper secondary: `#55565e`.

The existing variable named `--cyan` now holds copper for compatibility with the shared foundation. Geometry, spacing and contrast establish hierarchy, not decorative badges or repeated card grids.

## Motion, resilience and performance

The native canvas uses capped frame rates (30fps desktop, 24fps small screens) and bounded backing resolution (DPR 1.5 / 1.25). It pauses when offscreen, hidden, manually paused or device-reduced-motion is active. A static CSS fallback remains if canvas is unavailable. The controls stay synchronized and use no storage. No autoplay video, tracking, remote fonts, framework, animation dependency, scroll hijacking or pointer effect is introduced.

The existing native disclosures, accessible tab controls, image dialog, Escape behavior, language query and mobile navigation remain. Content and real downloads are available without JavaScript. Focus, selection, forced-colors and reduced-motion states are styled. English and Hebrew must be reviewed at desktop and narrow mobile widths.

## Research informing the direction

These are design observations, not measured conversion claims:

- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve): cinematic product presentation backed by real workspace evidence.
- [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/download/): clear free/open-source identity and platform-specific prerequisites.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/): an understandable workflow from source to output.
- [CapCut Desktop](https://www.capcut.com/tools/desktop-video-editor): a consistent download action after meaningful product evidence.
- [NN/g homepage principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/): establish purpose and the primary next step quickly.
- [W3C pause/stop/hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) and [animation from interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions): visible control and respect for reduced motion.
- [web.dev animation guidance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): constrain rendering work and stop unnecessary motion.

The requested Impeccable and project design guidance informed the work. The launcher was unavailable, so existing project context and the craft floor were read directly. No conversion lift, physical-device acceptance or current-app visual qualification is claimed. See the validation record for actual checks.
